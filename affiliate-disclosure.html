Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 2026

In plain language: some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up or buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is one of the ways we keep every calculator on SmartToolsHub free to use.

Why we're telling you this

The Federal Trade Commission requires websites to clearly disclose paid or commission-based relationships with the brands they link to. This page is that disclosure, and it applies to every page on toolshubfree.com.

Where affiliate links appear

You'll find affiliate links in a few places: product suggestions on our home-improvement calculators (FloorCalc Pro, HomeFixCost, HomeStyle AI, MoneyMap Pro), and links to financial services on our money-related pages (PayCalc Pro, HomeBuy Calc, the Debt Payoff Calculator, and our state and cost-of-living guides). These are typically labeled "Advertisement" or appear in a clearly separated product/services block, not woven into the calculator results themselves.

Programs we participate in

  • Amazon Associates Program — as an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases when you click an Amazon link from this site.
  • CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction) — an affiliate network through which we link to select financial and home-services companies.
  • Individual affiliate or referral relationships with specific companies whose products or services we link to, which may include mortgage, credit, auto, and home-services brands.

Companies we've linked to through these programs include (but aren't limited to) LendingTree, Rocket Mortgage, Better Mortgage, Credit Karma, TurboTax, H&R Block, The Zebra, Experian, Zillow, Carvana, CarMax, Thumbtack, and various home-improvement retailers. This list can change as our partnerships change — this page describes the type of relationship, not a fixed roster of every partner at every moment.

This does not change our calculators

Affiliate relationships never change the math a calculator produces. Your paycheck estimate, home affordability number, or debt payoff timeline is calculated the same way whether or not you ever click an ad or affiliate link on the page. We don't accept payment to alter a calculation or to recommend a product as "best" without our own independent reasoning for the recommendation.
