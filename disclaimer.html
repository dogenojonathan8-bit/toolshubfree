Calculator results are estimates, not guarantees

Our calculators (paycheck, home affordability, car affordability, debt payoff, renovation cost, flooring cost, budgeting, and rent-vs-buy) use published formulas and publicly available data — for example, IRS tax brackets, Freddie Mac's average mortgage rate, and state tax tables — combined with the numbers you enter. Two people with the same income can get very different real outcomes.

What our estimates do not account for

  • Individual lender underwriting standards, credit score tiers, or loan approval decisions.
  • Your specific tax situation — deductions, credits, filing status nuances, or local/municipal taxes not built into a calculator.
  • Property-specific factors like condition, HOA rules, or insurance risk that change a homeowner's actual costs.
  • Day-to-day changes in mortgage rates, home prices, or material costs between the "last reviewed" date on a page and the day you use it.
